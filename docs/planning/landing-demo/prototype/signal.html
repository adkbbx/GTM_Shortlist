    How it works

    Three checks before anything reaches your shortlist.

    Every company is checked against what you actually sell, who you said you want, and what its own website says. When a check fails, the company drops out with the reason recorded.

    01

    Offer

    We read your site once and draft what you sell. You can edit every word before anything else happens.

    relay-studio.example · page savedCustomer onboarding workflow reviews for B2B software teams.Edit offer

    drops: different market

    02

    Target

    You confirm who counts as a fit and what to exclude. No company search runs before you do.

    • B2B software with an onboarding flow
    • A company-owned page describing it
    • Exclude consumer-only products

    While it runs

    Watch the work, not a spinner.

    Every search, page read and check is saved as it happens. You see what passed, what failed and why, with real timestamps. No made-up percentages, and you can leave the page at any time.

    Two ways to run it

    Open source core. Hosted if you want it.

    The research engine is open source. Run it with your own keys, or use the hosted version and keep your research in an account.

    Run it yourself

    • MIT-licensed web app, API and worker
    • Your own search and model keys
    • Research stays in your own database
    Read the setup guide →

    Use the hosted version

    • An account that keeps your saved research
    • Research credits instead of provider keys
    • An optional call to talk through your shortlist

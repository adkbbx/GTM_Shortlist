    How it works

    How a shortlist gets made.

    Six steps from your website to a shortlist you can check. You confirm the target before anything runs, and you make the final call.

    While it runs

    Watch the work, not a spinner.

    Every search, page read and check is saved as it happens. You see what passed, what failed and why, with real timestamps. No made-up percentages, and you can leave the page at any time.
